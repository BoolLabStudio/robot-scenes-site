Robot-Scenes Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Local game data

Robot-Scenes stores your completed levels, sound and haptic settings, remaining lives and refill deadline on your device. Reset Progression clears completed levels only. The app does not include advertising, tracking or third-party analytics SDKs, and does not collect or export beta play reports.

Game Center

Game Center is optional. If you sign in, Apple handles authentication and the app displays your Game Center name and connection status. This version does not upload your local progression to a leaderboard or provide cloud save through Game Center. You can manage your Game Center account in iOS Settings. Apple’s services are governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

Purchases

Apple processes the optional Unlimited Lives purchase. The app uses StoreKit to verify whether the purchase is active and to restore it. BoolLab Studio does not receive your payment card details through the app. Refunds and revoked purchases can remove the Unlimited Lives entitlement.

Support

If you contact boollabstudio.info@gmail.com, BoolLab Studio receives the email and any information or attachments you choose to send, and uses them to respond to your request. Please do not send passwords or payment details. You may request deletion of your support correspondence at that address, subject to any applicable retention obligations.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated as the app changes. Questions about privacy: boollabstudio.info@gmail.com.
