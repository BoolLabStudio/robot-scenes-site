Escape Circuit

A turn-based escape puzzle by BoolLab Studio.

Every move matters

Guide the runner to the exit. Avoid walls and your temporary trail, read the guard robots’ timing, open security doors, hold pressure plates, follow one-way passages, jump between paired teleporters, slide across ice, push crates, and time cyclic laser barriers. Explore 65 challenges at your own pace: nothing moves until you choose your next step.

Lives, hints and purchases

You start with five lives. A failed or abandoned attempt costs one life. At zero, all five return after 30 minutes. Free hints replenish over time and reveal the next three moves. Unlimited Lives and packs of hint credits are optional in-app purchases. There are no ads.
