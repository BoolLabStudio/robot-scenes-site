Robot-Scenes

A turn-based delivery puzzle by BoolLab Studio.

Every move matters

Guide your blue truck to the delivery zone. Avoid walls and your temporary trail, read the robots’ timing, and use pursuer collisions to open a path. Explore 25 challenges at your own pace: nothing moves until you choose your next step.

Lives and purchases

You start with five lives. A failed or abandoned attempt costs one life. At zero, all five return after 15 minutes. Unlimited Lives is an optional one-time purchase that removes the life limit and waiting period. There are no ads.
